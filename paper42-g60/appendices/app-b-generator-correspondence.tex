\section{Explicit generator correspondence}
\label{app:generator-correspondence}

This appendix records the deterministic four-generator correspondence
used by the explicit isomorphism certificate
\[
\Phi\colon
\operatorname{Aut}(G60)
\longrightarrow
S_5\times_{C_2}D_8.
\]

The native generators are permutations of the fixed sixty-vertex
ordering. The model generators are pairs
\[
(\sigma,r^i s^j),
\]
where $\sigma\in S_5$ is written as an image array and the dihedral
coordinate is written as the pair $(i,j)$.

The fiber-product model uses the rotation-parity character
\[
\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)=i\bmod 2.
\]

\subsection{Generator orders and subgroup growth}

\subsection{Relation signature}

The first three generators satisfy
\[
g_0g_1=g_1g_0,
\]
\[
g_1g_2=g_2g_1,
\]
\[
g_0g_2\neq g_2g_0,
\]
and
\[
g_2^2=g_1.
\]

The mixed product orders involving the fourth generator are
\[
|g_3g_0|=5,
\qquad
|g_3g_1|=6,
\qquad
|g_3g_2|=6,
\]
together with
\[
|g_3g_0g_1|=10,
\]
\[
|g_3g_0g_2|=4,
\]
\[
|g_3g_1g_2|=6,
\]
and
\[
|g_3g_0g_1g_2|=12.
\]

The selected model generators satisfy the same complete relation
signature and generate all $480$ elements of the fiber-product model.
