\begin{abstract}
We determine the full automorphism group of the connected quartic
sixty-vertex graph $\mathrm{AT4val}[60,6]$, denoted $G60$.

Using the exact native graph and its canonical two-fold quotient
$G60 \to G30$, we construct all lifts of the automorphism group of the
quotient and obtain a subgroup of order $480$. An independent exhaustive
enumeration of the native graph also returns exactly $480$ automorphisms.
Every native automorphism preserves the quotient fibers and centralizes
the deck involution, so the lifted subgroup is the full automorphism
group.

We determine the internal group structure:
the center has order $2$, the derived subgroup is isomorphic to
$A_5 \times C_2$, the second derived subgroup is isomorphic to $A_5$,
and the abelianization is isomorphic to $C_2 \times C_2$.
We then identify the full group as the fiber product
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G60)
\cong
S_5 \times_{C_2} D_8,
\]
where the sign character on $S_5$ is matched with the quotient character
of $D_8$ having kernel a Klein four subgroup.

Finally, we construct an explicit bijection between the $480$ native
automorphisms and the $480$ elements of the fiber-product model and
verify all $230400$ ordered products. The result therefore supplies both
an abstract classification and a complete computational certificate.
\end{abstract}
