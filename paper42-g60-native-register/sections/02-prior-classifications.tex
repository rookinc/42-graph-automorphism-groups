\section{Prior Classifications and Imported Results}

The present paper begins from two completed companion classifications.
Those papers establish the ambient graph constructions and abstract
automorphism groups used here. This section records exactly which
results are imported and distinguishes them from the new structural
claims proved below.

\subsection{The canonical bipartite double cover}

Let \(P\) denote the Petersen graph and let
\[
Y=L(P)
\]
be its line graph. The first companion paper studies
\[
X=KC(Y)\cong Y\times K_2,
\]
the canonical bipartite double cover of \(L(P)\)
\cite{CaveCanonicalBipartite2026}.

That paper proves that \(X\) is a connected bipartite quartic graph
with thirty vertices and sixty edges. Its natural covering
construction displays a subgroup
\[
S_5\times C_2
\leq
\operatorname{Aut}(X),
\]
where the \(S_5\)-factor is lifted from
\(\operatorname{Aut}(L(P))\) and the \(C_2\)-factor is generated by
the natural deck involution.

The visible subgroup is not the full automorphism group. Through the
homogeneous-space realization
\[
V(X)\cong S_5/V_{4,\mathrm{even}},
\]
the companion paper identifies a complementary centralizer
\[
N_{S_5}(V_{4,\mathrm{even}})/V_{4,\mathrm{even}}
\cong S_3.
\]
The resulting full group is
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(X)\cong S_5\times S_3.
\]

The three involutions of the complementary \(S_3\) determine a
three-member automorphism orbit of regular two-fold quotient frames.
Each frame has quotient isomorphic to \(L(P)\), and the stabilizer of
each selected frame is
\[
S_5\times C_2.
\]
The three frame partitions are pairwise block-disjoint and together
contain forty-five distinct unordered vertex pairs.

The first companion paper also places \(X\) in the four-element
\(S_5\)-fixed binary-cover square over \(L(P)\). The present paper does
not import any claim that the exhibited three quotient frames exhaust
all regular \(L(P)\)-quotient frames of \(X\).

\subsection{The earlier \(G_{60}\) automorphism classification}

The second companion paper studies the distinct connected quartic
sixty-vertex graph
\[
G_{60}\cong \operatorname{AT4val}[60,6]
\]
\cite{CaveAT4Automorphism2026}.

It begins from a canonical fixed-point-free involution \(a\). The
thirty two-element \(a\)-orbits define a quotient
\[
G_{60}\longrightarrow G_{30}=G_{60}/\langle a\rangle.
\]
The quotient automorphism group has order \(240\), and every quotient
automorphism has exactly two lifts to \(G_{60}\). This constructs a
subgroup of order \(480\).

An independent exhaustive enumeration on the exact sixty-vertex graph
also returns exactly \(480\) automorphisms. The lifted and independently
enumerated permutation sets agree exactly. Every native automorphism
preserves the \(a\)-fiber partition and centralizes \(a\).

The internal invariants established there include
\[
Z(\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60}))\cong C_2,
\]
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})'
\cong A_5\times C_2,
\]
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})''
\cong A_5,
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})_{\mathrm{ab}}
\cong C_2\times C_2.
\]

The abstract group is identified as the fiber product
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
\cong
S_5\times_{C_2}D_8,
\]
where the sign character
\[
\operatorname{sgn}:S_5\longrightarrow C_2
\]
is matched with a quotient character
\[
\chi_{V_4}:D_8\longrightarrow C_2
\]
whose kernel is a Klein four-group.

That identification is certified by an explicit bijection between the
\(480\) native automorphisms and the \(480\) elements of the
fiber-product model. All
\[
480^2=230400
\]
ordered products are verified.

The earlier paper therefore determines the full automorphism group
both abstractly and constructively. It leaves open the geometric
interpretation of the Klein-four kernel, the order-four dihedral
directions, and the parity coupling in the native graph.

\subsection{Relationship between the two companion graphs}

The thirty-vertex graph
\[
X=KC(L(P))
\]
is not the quotient
\[
G_{30}=G_{60}/\langle a\rangle.
\]
The companion papers distinguish them by their full automorphism
groups:
\[
|\operatorname{Aut}(X)|=720,
\qquad
|\operatorname{Aut}(G_{30})|=240.
\]
Thus equal vertex count and a shared relation to Petersen-derived
structures do not identify the two graphs.

The role of \(X\) in the present paper is comparative. Its enlarged
\(S_3\)-symmetry shows how a selected quotient frame can sit inside a
larger intrinsic frame geometry. The role of the earlier \(G_{60}\)
paper is foundational. It supplies the exact native automorphism group
whose internal register geometry is reconstructed here.

\subsection{Imported facts}

The present paper imports the following results without reproving their
full certificate chains:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \(G_{60}\) is the exact connected quartic graph
    \(\operatorname{AT4val}[60,6]\) with sixty vertices and
    \(120\) edges.

    \item The canonical involution \(a\) is fixed-point-free,
    central, and preserved by the full automorphism group.

    \item The complete native automorphism group has order \(480\).

    \item There is an explicit group isomorphism
    \[
    \operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
    \cong
    S_5\times_{C_2}D_8
    \]
    using the quotient character of \(D_8\) with Klein-four kernel.

    \item The companion graph
    \[
    X=KC(L(P))
    \]
    has automorphism group
    \[
    \operatorname{Aut}(X)\cong S_5\times S_3
    \]
    and a certified three-member orbit of regular
    \(L(P)\)-quotient frames.
\end{enumerate}

Whenever exact labeled permutations, graph edges, quotient maps, or
coordinate bridges are used below, they are read from the preserved
Project 42 artifacts rather than reconstructed from abstract group
invariants alone.

\subsection{New claims proved in this paper}

The following are not imported from either companion paper and form
the principal contribution of the present work:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item recovery of a free normal native Klein group
    \[
    V_4=\{1,a,b,ab\}
    \]
    acting on \(G_{60}\);

    \item identification of its fifteen four-vertex orbits and the
    quotient
    \[
    G_{60}/V_4\cong L(P);
    \]

    \item construction of the four character operators and proof of
    the distinguished split
    \[
    a\mid\{b,ab\};
    \]

    \item identification of quotient-pentagon holonomy and the
    twenty-four registered ten-cycles;

    \item exact fourth-moment recognition of registered closure and
    localization of the sixteen suppressed external returns;

    \item classification of the \(120\)-cycle frontier and its fifteen
    foreign Klein stabilizers;

    \item recovery of five chamber triples that are independent in
    \(L(P)\) and complete in the foreign-Klein incidence graph;

    \item identification of those triples as five three-edge Petersen
    matchings partitioning \(E(P)\);

    \item proof that the full automorphism group acts as \(S_5\) on
    the five matching blocks with kernel exactly the native \(V_4\);

    \item proof that the extension splits and admits the native
    semidirect-product realization
    \[
    \operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
    \cong
    V_4\rtimes_{\operatorname{sgn}}S_5.
    \]
\end{enumerate}

The last description is equivalent to the earlier fiber-product
classification, but it is earned here from native graph structure.
The purpose is therefore not to identify a different abstract group.
It is to explain how the already classified group is realized by
chambers, characters, matchings, holonomy, and stabilizers inside
\(G_{60}\).
