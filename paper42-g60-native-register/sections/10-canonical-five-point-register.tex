\section{The Canonical Five-Point Register}

The previous section identifies five three-chamber blocks and interprets
them as Petersen matchings under one explicit quotient coordinate
bridge. We now prove that the five-block system itself is preserved by
the full automorphism group and therefore belongs intrinsically to the
native graph.

\subsection{The five-block system}

Let
\[
\mathcal M
=
\{T_0,T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4\}
\]
be the partition of the fifteen native chambers into the five triples
\[
T_0=\{0,5,14\},
\]
\[
T_1=\{1,6,12\},
\]
\[
T_2=\{2,9,13\},
\]
\[
T_3=\{3,7,11\},
\]
and
\[
T_4=\{4,8,10\}.
\]

Each \(T_i\) is independent in the quotient carrier and complete in
the foreign-Klein incidence graph.

The complete native automorphism group acts on the fifteen chambers
because the native Klein group is normal.

For
\[
g\in\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60}),
\]
write
\[
\overline g
\]
for the induced permutation of the fifteen chambers.

Direct evaluation of all \(480\) native automorphisms shows that
\[
\overline g(T_i)\in\mathcal M
\]
for every \(i\).

\begin{theorem}
The five-block partition
\[
\mathcal M=\{T_0,T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4\}
\]
is invariant under the full automorphism group of \(G_{60}\).
\end{theorem}

This promotes the matching partition from a pattern observed in one
coordinate system to an intrinsic block system of the native graph.

\subsection{The induced five-point action}

The invariant block system defines a homomorphism
\[
\rho:
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal M)
\cong S_5.
\]

The complete census produces
\[
120
\]
distinct block permutations.

Since
\[
|S_5|=120,
\]
the image is the full symmetric group.

\begin{theorem}
The induced action of
\(\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})\) on the five matching blocks is
surjective:
\[
\rho(\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60}))=S_5.
\]
\end{theorem}

The element-order distribution in the five-point image is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
\text{order} & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6\\
\hline
\text{count} & 1 & 25 & 20 & 30 & 24 & 20
\end{array}
\]
which is the complete element-order profile of \(S_5\).

The image contains
\[
60
\]
even permutations and
\[
60
\]
odd permutations.

\subsection{Kernel of the five-point action}

An automorphism lies in
\[
\ker\rho
\]
precisely when it fixes every matching block setwise.

The exact kernel has order
\[
4.
\]

Direct comparison with the recovered native Klein group gives
\[
\ker\rho
=
V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}.
\]

\begin{theorem}
The native Klein group is exactly the kernel of the canonical
five-point action:
\[
\ker\rho=V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}.
\]
\end{theorem}

Consequently,
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})/V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}
\cong S_5.
\]

This quotient is not merely inferred from the abstract order
calculation
\[
480/4=120.
\]
It is realized by an explicit faithful action on the five matching
blocks.

\subsection{The exact sequence}

The five-point action yields the exact sequence
\[
1
\longrightarrow
V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
\overset{\rho}{\longrightarrow}
S_5
\longrightarrow
1.
\]

This sequence gives a native geometric interpretation of the
\(S_5\)-coordinate appearing in the earlier fiber-product
classification.

The quotient \(S_5\) permutes the five Petersen matching blocks. The
kernel \(V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}\) acts within the four native states of
each chamber and is invisible at the five-block level.

\subsection{Transitivity properties}

The induced \(S_5\)-action is two-transitive on the five blocks.

For any two ordered pairs of distinct blocks
\[
(T_i,T_j)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(T_k,T_\ell),
\]
there exists a native automorphism mapping the first pair to the
second.

Equivalently, the orbit of one ordered distinct block pair has size
\[
5\cdot4=20.
\]

The action is also transitive on the ten unordered pairs of distinct
blocks:
\[
\binom52=10.
\]

\begin{proposition}
The canonical five-point action is two-transitive and
two-homogeneous.
\end{proposition}

The induced action on the ten inter-block pairs is faithful. This
ten-pair geometry is separate from the fifteen foreign Klein groups,
which index pairs inside the five three-element blocks.

\subsection{Block stabilizers}

For each matching block \(T_i\), its setwise stabilizer in the full
automorphism group has order
\[
\frac{480}{5}=96.
\]

This agrees with the quotient calculation
\[
96
=
|V_4|\cdot|S_4|
=
4\cdot24,
\]
because the stabilizer of one point in the five-point \(S_5\)-action
is isomorphic to \(S_4\).

\begin{proposition}
For every matching block \(T_i\),
\[
|\operatorname{Stab}(T_i)|=96.
\]
Its image under \(\rho\) is the point stabilizer
\[
S_4\leq S_5,
\]
and its kernel is the native \(V_4\).
\end{proposition}

The subgroup fixing all three chambers of one matching block
individually has order
\[
16.
\]

Thus the quotient-level stabilizer of the three chambers contributes
a factor of four above the native kernel.

\subsection{The equivariant factorization}

The native vertex set now has three invariant levels.

First, there are
\[
5
\]
matching blocks.

Second, each matching block contains
\[
3
\]
native chambers.

Third, each chamber contains
\[
4
\]
vertices forming one regular orbit of the native Klein group.

Hence
\[
60
=
5\cdot3\cdot4.
\]

\begin{theorem}
The factorization
\[
60=5\cdot3\cdot4
\]
is realized equivariantly by the native graph as:
\[
5
\text{ invariant matching blocks},
\]
\[
3
\text{ quotient chambers per block},
\]
and
\[
4
\text{ native Klein states per chamber}.
\]
\end{theorem}

This is stronger than a numerical decomposition. Each factor is
identified by an invariant group action.

\subsection{Visible and hidden coordinates}

The five-point quotient records the visible block coordinate:
\[
\rho(g)\in S_5.
\]

The native Klein kernel records the hidden four-state coordinate:
\[
v\mapsto hv,
\qquad
h\in V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}.
\]

These coordinates are not yet proved to form an internal direct
product. The quotient action may act nontrivially on the kernel.

The next section determines the extension type and proves that the
coupling is exactly the sign action:
\[
a\mapsto a,
\qquad
b\leftrightarrow ab
\]
under odd permutations.

\subsection{Intrinsic content}

The Petersen edge labels used to display the five matchings remain
coordinate choices. The following structure is intrinsic:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item the free normal native Klein group;
    \item the fifteen chambers;
    \item the five invariant chamber blocks;
    \item the full \(S_5\)-action on those blocks;
    \item the identification of the native Klein group as its kernel.
\end{enumerate}

Thus the native graph itself carries a canonical five-point register,
even before any Petersen labels are assigned.
