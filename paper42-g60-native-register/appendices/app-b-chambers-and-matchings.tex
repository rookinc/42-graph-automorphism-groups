\section{The Fifteen Chambers and Five Petersen Matchings}

This appendix records the quotient-chamber indexing, the five recovered
three-chamber blocks, and their interpretation as Petersen matchings
under the explicit Project 42 coordinate bridge.

\subsection{The fifteen native chambers}

The free native Klein action partitions the sixty vertices into
fifteen four-vertex orbits:
\[
V(G_{60})
=
\bigsqcup_{i=0}^{14} C_i.
\]

Each chamber has the form
\[
C_i
=
\{v_i,a(v_i),b(v_i),ab(v_i)\}
\]
for one selected representative \(v_i\).

The exact four-vertex lists are stored in
\[
\texttt{native\_g60\_v4\_recovery\_052.json}.
\]

The chamber indices
\[
0,\ldots,14
\]
are deterministic certificate coordinates. They are not claimed to be
canonical labels in isolation.

\subsection{The quotient carrier}

The orbit quotient is
\[
G_{60}/V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}
\cong
L(P).
\]

Accordingly, each chamber index corresponds to one edge of the
Petersen graph under the selected coordinate bridge.

The quotient is quartic on fifteen vertices. Chamber adjacency means
that the corresponding Petersen edges share an endpoint.

These form a partition:
\[
\{0,\ldots,14\}

\[
\Gamma_{\mathrm{for}}[T_i]\cong K_3.
\]

\subsection{Foreign Klein indexing}

Within each chamber block
\[
T_i=\{u,v,w\},
\]
the three foreign Klein subgroups correspond to the three internal
pairs
\[
\{u,v\},
\qquad
\{u,w\},
\qquad
\{v,w\}.
\]

Hence the complete foreign Klein family is indexed by
\[
\coprod_{i=0}^{4}\binom{T_i}{2}.
\]

The count is
\[
5\binom32=15.
\]

Each foreign subgroup stabilizes eight frontier cycles and has a
\(4+4\) relay-middle split across its two endpoint chambers.

\subsection{Five-point action}

The full automorphism group preserves the set
\[
\mathcal M
=
\{T_0,T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4\}
\]
and induces all \(120\) permutations of these five blocks.

The action homomorphism is
\[
\rho:
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
\longrightarrow
S_5,
\]
with
\[
\ker\rho=V_4^{\mathrm{nat}}.
\]

Each block stabilizer has order
\[
96,
\]
and the subgroup fixing all three chambers in one block individually
has order
\[
16.
\]
