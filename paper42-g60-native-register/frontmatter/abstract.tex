\begin{abstract}
We determine the internal geometric structure carried by the native
Klein-four subgroup of the connected quartic graph
\[
G_{60}\cong \operatorname{AT4val}[60,6].
\]
Earlier work established
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
\cong S_5\times_{C_2}D_8
\]
by complete enumeration and an explicit multiplication-preserving
bijection. Here we recover the corresponding Klein kernel directly in
the native graph and identify the structures through which the
automorphism group acts.

A free normal subgroup
\[
V_4=\{1,a,b,ab\}
\]
partitions the sixty vertices into fifteen four-vertex chambers, with
quotient
\[
G_{60}/V_4\cong L(P),
\]
where \(P\) is the Petersen graph. The chambers admit an invariant
partition into five triples. Under the explicit Petersen coordinate
bridge, these triples are five pairwise disjoint three-edge matchings
that partition \(E(P)\). The full automorphism group preserves this
five-block system and induces the full symmetric group \(S_5\), with
kernel exactly the native \(V_4\).

The resulting extension splits:
\[
1\longrightarrow V_4
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
\longrightarrow S_5
\longrightarrow 1,
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G_{60})
\cong V_4\rtimes_{\operatorname{sgn}}S_5.
\]
The conjugation action fixes \(a\), while odd permutations exchange
\(b\) and \(ab\). This explains the symmetry-protected isospectrality
of the corresponding character sectors.

We further identify twenty-four registered ten-cycles arising from
nontrivial quotient-pentagon holonomy, an exact fourth-moment
recognition law, and a \(120\)-cycle frontier carrying fifteen
non-native Klein stabilizers. Their chamber-incidence graph is
isomorphic to five disjoint triangles. Together these results provide
an equivariant realization of
\[
60=5\cdot3\cdot4
\]
as five Petersen matching blocks, three chambers per block, and four
native Klein states per chamber.
\end{abstract}
