## Purpose

Record the literature basis for the orbital-configuration language used in
Project 42, especially the action of centralizers and normalizers on orbital
relations.

## Editorial conclusion

Do not present a newly named Centralizer Orbital Partition Theorem as an
original standalone theorem.

Use the established coherent-configuration language:

- the orbits of a permutation group on ordered pairs form its orbital, or
  Schurian, coherent configuration;
- a weak automorphism permutes the basis relations;
- a strong automorphism fixes every basis relation individually;
- an element of the normalizer, and therefore of the centralizer, permutes
  the orbital relations;
- if one orbital relation is uniquely characterized by preserved
  graph-isomorphism invariants, then that relation is fixed.

For Project 42, the intended application is:

1. the certified census gives one connected quartic self-paired orbital;
2. the centralizer acts by weak automorphisms of the orbital configuration;
3. connectedness and valency are preserved under relabeling;
4. uniqueness forces the distinguished orbital relation to be fixed;
5. therefore the centralizer acts by automorphisms of X.
