X = KC(L(P)),

the canonical bipartite double cover of the line graph of the Petersen
graph.

Its certified full automorphism group is

Aut(X) ~= S5 x S3

of order 720.

The newly completed classification concerns the distinct sixty-vertex
native graph

G60 ~= AT4val[60,6].

Its certified full automorphism group is

Aut(G60) ~= S5 x_(C2) D8

of order 480.

These results must not be combined as though they concern the same graph.

Editorial correction

Preserve paper42 as the manuscript about X = KC(L(P)).

Develop the G60 automorphism classification as a separate manuscript
within Project 42.

Earlier refresh notes proposing replacement of Sections 06-10 are
superseded by this scope correction.
