# Section 03 Review

Decision

Preserve Section 03 with minimal or no revision.

Reason

Section 03 establishes the construction of the canonical bipartite double
cover together with its fundamental graph invariants.

These results remain valid and form the foundation for the remainder of
the paper.

The completed automorphism classification does not alter the graph
construction or its certified invariants.

Editorial decision

Leave the construction, invariant calculations, and supporting
certification intact.

Only editorial improvements should be considered if they improve clarity.

No new theorem material should be introduced here.

The principal new mathematics remains reserved for Sections 06 through 10.
