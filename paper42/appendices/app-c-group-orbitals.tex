\subsection{Group certificate and boundaries}

The compact certificate verifies

\[
|\Aut(X)|=720,
\qquad
|\ker\rho|=120,
\qquad
|C_{\Aut(X)}(\ker\rho)|=6,
\]

with trivial intersection between the kernel and its centralizer. Their
product has order \(720\). The respective element-order profiles identify
the factors as \(S_5\) and \(S_3\), so

\[
\Aut(X)\cong S_5\times S_3.
\]

It also records

\[
|Z(\Aut(X))|=1,
\qquad
|\Aut(X)'|=180,
\qquad
|\Aut(X)^{\mathrm{ab}}|=4.
\]
