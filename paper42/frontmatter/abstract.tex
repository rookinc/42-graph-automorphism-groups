\begin{abstract}

Let \(P\) be the Petersen graph, let \(Y=L(P)\), and let

\[
X=\KC(Y)
\]

be the canonical bipartite double cover of its line graph. The graph \(X\)
is connected, bipartite, quartic, and has thirty vertices. Its covering
construction displays a subgroup \(S_5\times C_2\), but this is only the
stabilizer of one selected quotient frame.

We identify \(X\) independently as the unique connected member of the
certified quartic self-paired orbital census for the action of \(S_5\) on

\[
S_5/V_{4,\mathrm{even}}.
\]

The normalizer quotient

\[
N_{S_5}(V_{4,\mathrm{even}})/V_{4,\mathrm{even}}
\cong S_3
\]

acts through the centralizer of this coset action. Weak-automorphism
equivariance of orbital relations, together with certified uniqueness of
the connected quartic orbital, forces this complementary \(S_3\) to act
by graph automorphisms. An exact automorphism enumeration then gives

\[
\Aut(X)\cong S_5\times S_3.
\]

The three involutions of the complementary factor yield a three-member
automorphism orbit of regular \(L(P)\)-quotient frames. These frames are
pairwise block-disjoint and contain forty-five distinct unordered vertex
pairs. Finally, we place \(X\) in the four-element \(S_5\)-fixed binary
cohomology square over \(L(P)\). No assertion is made that every regular
\(L(P)\)-quotient frame belongs to the exhibited orbit.

\end{abstract}
