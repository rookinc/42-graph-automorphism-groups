The conceptual arguments in this paper are combined with exact finite
computations. This section records which statements depend on
certificates and which statements are deductions from them.

\subsection{Labeled construction}

All computations begin from one fixed labeled Petersen graph. Its edges
label the vertices of

\[
Y=L(P),
\]

and the vertices of the canonical bipartite double cover are labeled by

\[
V(Y)\times\Ftwo.
\]

Appendix~A gives a coordinate-free labeled model. The exact conversion to
the integer labels used in the machine-readable certificates is part of
the certificate packet and will be inserted verbatim during the revision
pass. No mathematical claim depends on the choice of integer labels.

\subsection{Conceptual deductions}

The following results are not separate searches:

\begin{itemize}

\item
The centralizer formula

\[
C_{\Sym(S_5/H)}(S_5)\cong N_{S_5}(H)/H
\]

is proved conceptually.

\item
The complementary \(S_3\) is obtained from the normalizer quotient and the
certified uniqueness of the connected quartic orbital relation.

\item
The subgroup

\[
S_5\times S_3
\]

is constructed conceptually. The automorphism enumeration is used only
as the matching upper bound.

\item
The three quotient graphs and their stabilizers follow by conjugacy after
the single certified alignment of \(\tau_0\) with the complementary
factor.

\item
The forty-five-pair count follows conceptually from semiregularity of the
centralizer action, although the explicit labeled census remains an
independent check.

\item
The center, derived subgroup, and abelianization follow directly from the
direct-product theorem.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Current nonclaims}

The paper does not currently claim:

\begin{itemize}

\item that every regular \(L(P)\)-quotient frame of \(X\) belongs to
\(\frorbit\);

\item a conceptual classification of the four quartic double cosets;

\item a representation-theoretic derivation of the fixed cohomology basis;

\item a standard external graph name for \(X\); or

\item that multiple quotient frames characterize the all-one cover class
among all invariant covers.

\end{itemize}
